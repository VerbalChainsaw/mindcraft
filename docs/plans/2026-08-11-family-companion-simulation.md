**Purpose:** exercise the companion as it might be used by a twelve-year-old

## Player-visible outcome

IronSuiteProof should feel useful, attentive, truthful, and pleasant to play
beside across a mixed family session. It should distinguish conversation from
work, follow ordinary requests through the existing deterministic capability
engine, respect both players and Operator Stop, preserve shared structures,
finish or fail honestly, return to the players, and settle calmly afterward.

This is not a capability certification matrix. Each session is a broad natural
play arc. The first material player-visible failure selects the repair. Passed
mechanics remain frozen unless the session supplies contradictory live
evidence.

## Simulated family

- `KidPlayer` represents the twelve-year-old: curious, informal, easily
  distracted, and likely to change plans after noticing something interesting.
- `DadPlayer` represents his father: practical, concerned with preserving the
  base, and willing to clarify, redirect, or Stop work.
- Both are disposable offline-mode Mineflayer clients acting only as ordinary
  players. They do not become agents, planners, fixtures, or execution owners.
- Their movement uses native Pathfinder. Their requests reach IronSuiteProof
  through ordinary Minecraft chat.

## Session program

### Session 1 — Saturday base day

Join IronSuiteProof, converse briefly, ask it to accompany the family, then
request a useful common-resource contribution that reuses existing carried or
nearby infrastructure. Change direction once if ordinary play offers a reason,
ask it to return, and finish with Stop and calm idle.

### Session 2 — Family mining trip

Travel together toward an ordinary cave or mining area. Ask for useful iron,
coal, or torch support without manufacturing scarcity or danger. Observe tool
selection and replacement, follow distance, safe return, truthful progress,
and reaction to naturally occurring threats.

### Session 3 — Clubhouse or workshop help

Ask for a modest functional family build near an authorized work area. The kid
supplies the imaginative purpose; Dad supplies preservation, access, lighting,
and storage constraints. Existing structures and paths must remain intact.

### Session 4 — Farm and animals afternoon

Visit an existing farm, harvest only mature crops, replant, reuse authorized
storage, and interact with animals only when the real world already supplies a
safe enclosure and required food.

### Session 5 — Explore, remember, and guide

Leave home without a rigid destination, notice real landmarks, remember one
useful observation, guide the family back to it, then return home.

### Session 6 — Change of plans and quiet companionship

Begin one useful task, redirect or Stop it naturally, then spend time walking
and talking without assigning more work. The companion must not treat ordinary
conversation as orders or invent chores to fill silence.

## Observation and tagging

The online flight recorder automatically captures high-value action failures,
bounded request-correlated action successes, director failures, deaths, stalls,
and terminal goal boundaries. The simulated players send a standalone `WTF`
bookmark when behavior appears suspicious even before a formal failure,
including:

- ignored tools or inventory;
- repeated or nonsensical movement;
- failure to reach an obviously adjacent object;
- false progress or completion language;
- ignored Stop or changed instructions;
- destructive or inconsiderate world interaction;
- technically successful behavior that feels distinctly unlike a companion.

Acceptance also includes gameplay judgment, not only terminal task state. Tag
needless detours, partial trees, ugly single-block scaffolds, open holes near
shared areas, avoidable terrain scars, wasteful tool choice, failure to switch
or replace tools, repeated identical attempts, abandonment of recoverable
work, and technically valid routes that no sensible player would choose. Each
tag records the exact action and location, the saner alternative, and whether
the owner is target judgment, project policy, or delegated Mineflayer mechanics.

Each bookmark is interpreted with its surrounding canonical state and dialogue;
it is not automatically a defect verdict.
